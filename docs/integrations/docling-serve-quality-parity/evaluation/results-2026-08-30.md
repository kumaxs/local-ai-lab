# Document quality evaluation — 2026-08-30

This report records the offline acceptance evidence through engineering commit
`220f9f3` (including the completion-audit fixes in `20b9a7a`).
PDF binaries, rendered pages, and conversion outputs remain
outside Git. Counts are reported as `high-confidence / ambiguous`.

## Changes under evaluation

- picture-contained OCR is blocked from the ordinary body collector and the
  special algorithm-grouping path;
- the PDF inventory recognizes a definition/procedure algorithm only when a
  bounded title contains the algorithm term and at least three numbered or
  control steps support the classification; an explicit continuation cue is
  required to cross a page boundary;
- the final adapter emits bounded region records and fails on unresolved
  critical evidence;
- every promoted structural region is rebound to the final document node,
  normalized body identity, source PDF digest, page/bbox, and kind-constrained
  source asset; missing, partial, duplicate, extra, or unsafe evidence fails
  closed;
- algorithm manifest bindings must match every contributor in
  `algorithm_blocks.json`, including node kind, body hash, page/bbox, union
  geometry, and page span; inline-math identity retains operators, relation
  signs, punctuation, and Unicode math symbols and rejects truncated source
  text;
- independent table-cell geometry detects a one-row cell crossing another row
  center, repeated tall numeric cells that flatten visible subrows, empty grids
  without an explicit fallback, and incomplete occupancy of the declared grid;
- an algorithm contributor declared as `table_grid` now independently requires
  a positive bounded grid, non-empty semantic cells, valid offsets/spans, no
  overlap, and complete occupancy. The ordinary empty-table visual fallback
  cannot promote an empty or sparse algorithm contributor;
- the unbound string-only "axis tail" sweep was removed after completion audit
  showed that it could erase legitimate captions or short prose. Axis/chart
  material is quarantined only when source structure, page/bbox, picture
  overlap, repetition, or other bound evidence supports that decision.

No implementation rule uses a filename, paper title, sample ID, known SHA-256,
or literal text unique to one paper.

## Unverified boundary

No service or Docker process was started in this workstream. The repository
operating rules require explicit authorization before starting them, and no
Docling/backend/API listener was present during inspection. Consequently the
four calibration PDFs have inventory and visual calibration evidence but no
fresh end-to-end conversion/region sidecars from the new code. A release or
production-readiness claim remains blocked until the user authorizes service
startup and the required CN plus English/two-column smoke conversions run.

Two interpretation limits remain explicit. First, a zero algorithm/code/table
inventory count with parser proof marked `healthy` proves only that the
inventory completed without internal proof errors; it is not independent
visual evidence that the PDF contains no such structure. Second, inline math
currently preserves operator-bearing source identity and provides bound
anchors/crops, but ordinary HTML/Markdown prose is not universally rewritten
as inline MathML or a semantic math span. Both require fresh visual/E2E review
before a stronger recall or semantic-rendering claim.
