# Document quality evaluation — 2026-08-30

This report records the 2026-08-30 acceptance evidence through engineering
commit `5ad1aa8` (including formula/region sealing in `fa8d9ba` and the hardened
macOS lifecycle in `5ad1aa8`). PDF binaries, rendered pages, service state, and
conversion outputs are deliberately excluded from Git. Inventory counts are
reported as `high-confidence / ambiguous`.

## Scope and decision

The requested Web UI, runtime lifecycle configuration, region evidence, and
deployment lifecycle work is implemented and test-covered. A source-built
Docker job also completed end to end. The document-quality release decision is
nevertheless **fail closed / not production ready**:

- diverse fresh direct-deployment set: `1 / 6` strict passes;
- freshly reconverted old regression set: `0 / 10` strict passes;
- all old inventory page and structure counts remain equal to their locked
  baseline, so no count-level regression was introduced;
- failures are quality/evidence decisions, not converter crashes or timeouts.

No implementation rule uses a filename, title, sample ID, known SHA-256, or
literal text unique to one paper.

## Changes under evaluation

- picture-contained OCR, figures, and repeated header/footer material are
  quarantined from ordinary body collection and algorithm grouping;
- formulas, inline math, tables, algorithms, and code must bind the submitted
  PDF digest, page/bbox, source asset, final node, and normalized body identity;
- inline-math comparison retains operators, relations, punctuation, and
  Unicode mathematical symbols and rejects truncated candidates;
- algorithm/code surfaces preserve preformatted whitespace and indentation;
  cross-page algorithms require real evidence for every contributing page;
- table topology independently checks spans, overlap, row crossing/collapse,
  and complete occupancy of the declared grid;
- the final region sidecar is deterministic and bounded; unresolved critical
  evidence, truncation, unsafe paths, or partial publication fails closed;
- the unbound string-only axis-tail deletion heuristic was removed. Figure or
  chart text is suppressed only with source structure, repetition, page/bbox,
  picture overlap, or equivalent bound evidence;
- accepted private `formula_service` output, or a fully gated/rollback-protected
  `apply-all` result, owns only the final formula surface. Route A remains the
  non-formula document baseline.

## Interpretation limits and next quality gate

A healthy zero algorithm/code/table inventory means the independent inventory
completed consistently; it does not visually prove absence. Inline math now has
operator-preserving identity and source anchors/crops, but ordinary prose is not
universally rewritten into inline MathML or a dedicated semantic span.

Before a production-quality release, generic fixes must reduce the unresolved
formula/inline binding in FRCD, CN, PP-FormulaNet, and Transformers GNN; prove
remaining picture/main-flow and table-topology cases; and avoid Donut sidecar
truncation. The complete fresh six, old ten, and stored 23-paper replay must then
be rerun with no inventory regression and no unresolved critical regions.
